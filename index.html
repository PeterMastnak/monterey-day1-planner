    iHow the times are calculated
    Waves: shown from the forecast but they only slow the vessel if you set "speed loss per metre of waves" (no validated wave model for this vessel; ask the operator). Keep a buffer. Live uses the Open-Meteo forecast for the chosen date: Copernicus global surface currents (~8 km, tides included), 10 m wind and gusts, and wave height. Each leg is stepped every 300 m: the vessel crabs into the cross-current, and the current plus 2 % of the wind adds to or subtracts from its speed along the track, at the forecast hour it is there. The coarse current grid misses small nearshore eddies.

    Saved runs work offline: the route planner's earlier runs with NOAA WCOFS currents + 2 % wind from Sep 27, 29 and 30, lined up with a 10:00 launch, taking the slowest of the three for each leg. Passes stay 200 m off the receiver, outside the 100 m keep-out; every leg is ≥ 190 m from every receiver and over water > 3 m deep. Battery is a rough estimate (3.5 kWh standard pack; energy per km grows with speed²). Official speeds: 2 kn cruise, 6 kn max; 8 kn is a third-party claim. Sunset 18:36, sunrise Tue 07:11 (PDT).
