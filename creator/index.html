    Vessel and battery
    Propulsion: 3.5 kWh per 100 nm at 2 kn (manufacturer), growing with speed². Solar follows the sun and the forecast cloud cover (30 % assumed without a forecast). Route-following overhead adds distance for steering around the line and rounding waypoints.
    Operator export settings
    Passes export as GOTO waypoints, sampling stops as LOITER with duration and radius. Every bend of the water path is its own waypoint.

    How routes and times are calculated
    Each leg is the shortest path through water deeper than 3 m (NOAA CRM bathymetry, ~90 m grid) that stays at least 150 m from every receiver (and out of kelp if you choose), straightened so it only bends where something is in the way. Receiver stops are the stand-off point 200 m from the receiver toward deeper water. A ramp inside a harbor joins the nearest grid water cell with a short straight line, so check harbor exits by eye.
    Still water: distance (plus route-following overhead) / speed. Live forecast: Open-Meteo, Copernicus surface currents (~8 km, tides included), 10 m wind and gusts, waves and cloud cover, stepped every 300 m at the hour the vessel is there. The vessel crabs into the cross-current; current plus 2 % of the wind adds to or subtracts from its speed along the track; waves slow it only by the percentage you set. The likely range shows ±10 % speed.
    Kelp: CDFW 2016 statewide canopy survey (surface + subsurface), rasterised to the routing grid; beds change every year, so treat it as a guide. MPAs: CDFW California Marine Protected Areas [ds582]. Battery is an estimate; calibrate it and the speed with a real GPS track via Track vs plan after the first mission.
